# 💡 Lighting System Redesign & Heat Load Analysis  
### Electrical Engineering Computer Laboratory

## 📌 Overview  
This project presents a comprehensive redesign of the lighting system for an Electrical Engineering Computer Laboratory, along with a detailed heat load calculation to determine the required HVAC capacity.

The objective was to improve **visual comfort, energy efficiency, and overall indoor environmental quality** by integrating modern lighting design practices with thermal analysis.

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Evaluate the performance of the existing lighting system  
- Design an optimized LED lighting layout  
- Achieve required illuminance levels and improve uniformity  
- Reduce energy consumption and improve system efficacy  
- Perform a complete heat load calculation  
- Select a suitable air conditioning system based on calculated cooling demand  

---

## 🏗️ Project Workflow  

### 1. Laboratory Modeling  
- Developed a **3D model using DIALux Evo**  
- Incorporated accurate room dimensions, furniture layout, and architectural features  
- Considered reflection factors, maintenance factor, and real operating conditions  

---

### 2. Existing Lighting Analysis  
- Simulated current fluorescent lighting system  
- Key findings:
  - Average illuminance below required levels  
  - Poor uniformity causing uneven light distribution  
  - High glare affecting visual comfort  
  - Low luminous efficacy compared to modern systems  

---

### 3. Proposed Lighting Design  
- Replaced legacy fluorescent fixtures with **high-efficiency LED panels**  
- Optimized luminaire placement and spacing  
- Key improvements:
  - Achieved required illuminance levels (>300 lux)  
  - Significant increase in luminous efficacy  
  - Reduction in total power consumption (~40%)  
  - Improved lighting quality and reduced glare  

---

## 📊 Performance Comparison  

| Parameter | Existing System | Proposed System | Improvement |
|----------|---------------|----------------|------------|
| Avg. Illuminance | ~294 lx | ~346 lx | ↑ Improved |
| Power Consumption | ~1240 W | ~748 W | ↓ Reduced |
| Luminous Efficacy | ~54.8 lm/W | ~120.5 lm/W | ↑ Significant |
| Uniformity | 0.18 | 0.20 | Slight improvement |

---

## ❄️ Heat Load Calculation  

A detailed heat load analysis was conducted to determine the cooling requirements of the laboratory.

### 🔹 Considered Heat Sources  
- **Internal Loads**
  - Occupants  
  - Computers and equipment  
  - Lighting system  

- **External Loads**
  - Heat transfer through walls  
  - Glass windows and doors (solar gain)  
  - Air infiltration  

---

### 🔹 Results  
- **Total Heat Load:** ~23.1 kW  
- **Cooling Requirement:** ~78,869 BTU/h  

---

## 🧊 HVAC System Selection  
- Proposed System:  
  - **2 × 48,000 BTU/h inverter-type air conditioning units**  

- Key considerations:
  - Efficient load handling with safety margin  
  - Uniform air distribution  
  - Energy-efficient operation (inverter technology)
